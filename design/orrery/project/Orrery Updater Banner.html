Orrery Updater

orrery-updater.exe

A frameless, always-on-top mini-window shown while the app is closed and the new version installs. No close or minimize chrome — it's a viewer, not a controller. Lifetime is 3–10 seconds with zero interaction; the only decision point is the failed state.

Happy path · live

The 3-second life

Preparing waits for the old app to exit, the installer drives a real 0→100%, the bar pulses full, and the window dissolves the instant the new Orrery appears. It loops here for preview.

Every state

Window — 340 × 110, frameless, rounded 14px, drop shadow, always-on-top, centered, not resizable, dark-only.
Progress — MSI emits a real percent (clamped to move only forward); NSIS emits none, so the bar stays indeterminate.
Type — Space Grotesk titles · JetBrains Mono status & tabular percent. Accent #a855f7, danger #ff5d6c.
